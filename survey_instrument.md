# Kano Survey Instrument (Simulated Pilot)

**This instrument was used only to generate synthetic responses. No real respondents answered it.**

Product: a fictional grocery delivery app.

## How each feature is asked

Every feature is asked twice, as a pair of questions.

**Functional question:** "If the app had [feature], how would you feel?"

**Dysfunctional question:** "If the app did NOT have [feature], how would you feel?"

## Answer scale (the same five choices for every question)

1. I like it that way
2. It must be that way
3. I am neutral
4. I can live with it that way
5. I dislike it that way

## Features tested

| Short name | Description shown to respondents |
| --- | --- |
| Live order tracking | See your shopper and driver on a map while your order is on its way |
| Approve substitutions | Approve or reject each substitute item before it is added to your order |
| Delivery within 30 minutes | Orders arrive in 30 minutes or less |
| Loyalty rewards | Earn points on every order that can be redeemed for discounts |
| Recipe suggestions | Get recipe ideas based on what is in your cart |
| Plastic-free packaging | Orders arrive in compostable or reusable packaging only |
| Chat with shopper | Message your shopper directly while they are shopping |
| Price-match guarantee | The app matches a lower price found at a competing store |

## How the answer pairs are classified

The standard Kano evaluation table maps each pair of answers to a category.

| Functional \\ Dysfunctional | Like | Must-be | Neutral | Live with | Dislike |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Like | Q | A | A | A | O |
| Must-be | R | I | I | I | M |
| Neutral | R | I | I | I | M |
| Live with | R | I | I | I | M |
| Dislike | R | R | R | R | Q |

Categories: **A** = Attractive (a delighter), **O** = One-dimensional (more is better), **M** = Must-be (expected), **I** = Indifferent, **R** = Reverse (the customer prefers not having it), **Q** = Questionable (a contradictory answer pair).

## For a real study

A real version would add a screener, demographic questions, a question asking how important each feature is, and a short open-ended question asking what the respondent would most like to change. It would be fielded to a quota sample of actual customers.
